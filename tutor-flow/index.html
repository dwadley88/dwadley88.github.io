Feedback Exporter

Bold the phrase. It arrives as a key expression.

While you write a paraphrase in the lesson doc, bold the exact phrase you want the student to walk away with. One click — or one keystroke, with no hunting for Ringle’s own small control — sends it across to the Feedback Report.

Tutor Flow reads the bolding and pulls out each bolded phrase as its own key expression, split out rather than lumped in. Nothing to copy, nothing to retype, no second pass.

I’ve personally cut my feedback time in half.

Formatting Shortcuts

Write feedback at typing speed.

Feedback docs written the way tutors actually think — asterisks for bold, brackets for corrections, no reaching for a toolbar.

  • *word*becomes bold as you close the second asterisk. No toolbar click.
  • [ ] —bracket and hyphen shortcuts mark up insertions and corrections inline, the way you’d mark up a paper by hand.
  • “ ”quotation marks pair themselves as you type.
  • hla highlight toggle marks a phrase for emphasis instantly.
  • ⌄active-line centering keeps the line you’re writing comfortably in view instead of drifting to the bottom of the screen.

Calendar Defender

Offer a recurring slot with confidence.

Connect your Google Calendar and your existing commitments appear right on Ringle’s booking grid, so the time you’re already busy stops being bookable.

  • ·Separate Regular and Holiday overlays, each with its own colour.
  • ·Manual blocks for one-off unavailability.
  • ·Recurring offers — “every Monday 4:00–4:40pm” — are checked against weeks of your calendar at once, and clashes are flagged before you offer the slot. No paging through the calendar week by week.
Student Tracker

Your calendar and your students, handled carefully.

Calendar access is read-only, through your own Google account.

Calendar data is used to draw overlays on Ringle’s booking grid. It isn’t shared with Ringle and it isn’t sent anywhere else.

Student notes and records stay in your own browser.

The only thing that leaves your browser is license verification.
