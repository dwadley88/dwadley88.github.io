Formatting Shortcuts

Write feedback at typing speed.

Feedback docs written the way tutors actually mark up a paper — asterisks for bold, brackets and a hyphen for corrections, all without reaching for a toolbar or a mouse.

  • *word*becomes bold the moment you close the second asterisk — Tab or Enter both trigger it, and Backspace deletes the pair in one stroke.
  • [word]turns into an in-line correction the instant you type the closing bracket, hit Tab, or hit Enter — the same orange markup Ringle’s own correction tool produces.
  • -wordplus Tab fires Ringle’s own correction suggestion on that word automatically, then a second Tab jumps you cleanly out of it so you keep typing without reaching for the mouse.
  • “ ”quotation marks pair themselves as you type, Tab hops you past the closing mark, and Enter inside a quote drops to the next line instead of splitting it in two.
  • ⌃/⌘ 3 · 4toggle yellow highlighting or cycle through pink and green, from the top row or the numpad — no mouse trip to the toolbar swatches.
  • ⌄active-line centering keeps the line you’re writing comfortably in view instead of drifting to the bottom of the screen.

Feedback Exporter

Bold the phrase. It arrives as a key expression.

While you write a paraphrase in the lesson doc, bold the exact phrase you want the student to walk away with, then click the purple arrow — the same one Ringle already puts there — to send the block to the Feedback Report.

Tutor Flow reads the bolding and pulls out each bolded phrase as its own key expression, split out rather than lumped in. Nothing to copy, nothing to retype, no second pass. Try it below.

Calendar Defender

Connect your Google Calendar and your existing commitments appear right on Ringle’s booking grid, so the time you’re already busy stops being bookable.

  • ·Separate Regular and Holiday overlays, each with its own colour.
