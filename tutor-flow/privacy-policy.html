Privacy Policy — Ringle Tutor Toolkit

Last updated: 2026-08-31

Contact: support@nativeflow.me

Overview

Ringle Tutor Toolkit is a Chrome extension that helps tutors on the Ringle platform manage lessons, track student progress, and streamline in-lesson workflows. This policy explains what data the extension handles, where it lives, and what control you have over it.

The short version: your lesson content, student notes, and diagnostic logs stay on your own computer. We do operate one small backend — the Tutor Flow License Server — but it only ever sees your license key and subscription status, never your lesson or student data. See "Where data leaves your device" below for the full picture.

What data the extension collects

While you use the extension during Ringle tutoring sessions, it may store the following locally on your device:

  • Student information you enter or that appears during lessons — student names, private notes, ratings, and lesson history.
  • Lesson content — question text, tutor responses, and paraphrase data generated during feedback workflows.
  • Draft content — in-progress text from the lesson editor, saved locally so it isn't lost if the page reloads.
  • Diagnostic and error logs — a rolling log of extension activity (errors, warnings, and message activity between the extension's own components) used for troubleshooting. Entries are capped at a fixed count and the oldest are dropped automatically as new ones arrive. Known-sensitive values, such as your license key, are masked before they're written to this log. Beyond that specific masking, entries are not currently filtered field-by-field, so other message content can appear in a log entry if it's part of an error or warning; log entries are size-limited and never sent anywhere unless you choose to export the file yourself.
  • License and subscription status — your license key, activation status, and expiry date, stored locally so the extension knows whether it's activated. The key is masked (only the last few characters shown) anywhere it's displayed or logged.
  • Google Calendar data — if you connect your Google account, the extension reads your calendar list and events to display your schedule, and reads your Google name and email address to show which account is connected. It does not read, write, or modify anything else in your Google account.

The extension does not collect analytics, usage telemetry, advertising identifiers, or browsing history outside of the Ringle platform pages it operates on.

Where this data is stored

Your lesson content, student notes, drafts, and diagnostic logs are stored locally in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own device. None of that is transmitted to, or stored on, any server we operate — it never leaves your machine except in the situations below. The one exception is your license key and subscription status, which we do need to verify against our license server; see below for exactly what that involves.

Where data leaves your device

Data leaves your device in three situations:

  1. Google Calendar API calls (googleapis.com) — read-only requests to display your calendar and connected-account name/email, sent only when you have connected your Google account, and only to Google's servers.
  2. Ringle's own platform (ringletutor.com, ringleplus.com, ringleserver.com, and ringlefront.com subdomains) — the extension reads and writes data on Ringle's own pages as part of normal tutoring functionality, in the same way any browser extension interacts with the page it's installed on.
  3. The Tutor Flow License Server (a Cloudflare Worker we operate, at tutor-flow-license-server.native-flow-online.workers.dev) — your license key is sent here to activate and periodically re-verify your subscription. This server talks to Creem, our payment processor, on our behalf; it never sees your lesson content, student notes, or calendar data. It stores only what's needed to run licensing and an optional referral-reward program: your license key's associated customer ID, subscription/activation status, and (if you use a referral code) which customer referred which. It does not receive or store the content of your lessons, student records, or calendar.

No data is sent to any analytics service, advertiser, or third party beyond Google, Ringle, and our own license server as described above.

Your Google Account data

If you choose to connect your Google account, we request two scopes:

  • calendar.readonly — this powers "Calendar Defender," the feature that displays your existing Google Calendar events directly alongside your Ringle teaching schedule so you can immediately see and avoid double-booking. We request read-only access because the feature only needs to detect and display conflicts — it never creates, modifies, or deletes calendar events. Calendar data is read and rendered locally in the extension's own UI; it is not transmitted to, or stored on, any server we operate, and is not shared with third parties. We considered a narrower, per-calendar scope, but tutors need conflicts checked across every calendar they maintain (personal and professional), so calendar-level scoping wouldn't support the feature as designed.
  • userinfo.profile — this identifies which Google account is connected, so the extension can show you the correct account context and confirm the calendar being read belongs to the account you intended to connect. Your name and email are shown only inside the extension; no profile data is stored on our servers or shared with third parties.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the extension's settings; disconnecting revokes the extension's access via Google's own revocation endpoint immediately.
  • Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and your control over it

  • All locally stored data persists on your device until you remove it — either by uninstalling the extension, clearing your browser's extension storage, or using the extension's built-in tools.
  • Diagnostic and error logs are automatically capped and rotated: the oldest entries are dropped once the log reaches its size limit, so it can't grow without bound. You can clear the stored error log at any time from the extension's popup ("Clear stored errors").
  • Student tracker records (names, notes, ratings, and lesson history) can be cleared at any time from the extension's popup ("Reset all tracker data").
  • The extension includes an "Export error log" feature that saves a diagnostic file to your Downloads folder for troubleshooting purposes. This file is created locally and only exists if you explicitly choose to export it. Treat exported files the same as you would any file containing student information — store and delete them accordingly.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, and the extension has no capability to collect: payment information (Creem, our payment processor, handles that directly — we never see your card details), passwords, browsing history outside Ringle's platform, contacts, location data, or any data from websites other than the Ringle, Google, and license-server domains described above.

Children's privacy

The extension is a professional tool intended for use by adult tutors. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and, where the change is material, we will note it in the extension's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@nativeflow.me.
